Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026.

The Hub - Izk is a private tool with one user, its owner, and it runs on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public. The only Google account that connects to it is the owner's.

What the app reads

When the owner connects their YouTube account, the app asks for read-only access to YouTube (the youtube.readonly permission). With it, the app reads:

  • the videos the account has liked,
  • the items of one playlist the owner names,
  • public search results for the owner's projects.

The app cannot upload, edit, delete, rate or comment, and it does not read watch history, messages or any other Google data.

What it keeps, and where

  • For each video: its id and link, title, channel name, thumbnail, and the owner's own notes.
  • This is stored in a database on the owner's computer. It is not uploaded to any server run by the app, because the app has none.
  • The sign-in token is stored in the macOS Keychain on that computer.
  • Details that came from YouTube are refreshed once they are more than 30 days old, and search results the owner did not keep are reduced to the bare link after 30 days.

Who it is shared with

Nobody. The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not given to anyone else.

There is one case where a video's link leaves the computer: when the owner asks for an analysis of a reference video, the public link of that video is sent to Google's Gemini API to produce the analysis.

Removing access and data

  • The owner can disconnect YouTube inside the app, which revokes the sign-in token at Google and deletes it from the computer.
  • Access can also be removed at any time from the Google Account page for third-party connections.
  • Stored video details exist only on the owner's computer, where the owner can remove them at any time.

Google and YouTube terms

The app uses YouTube API Services. Using the YouTube connection means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

This website

This site is two static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. GitHub, as the host, may log visits as described in its own privacy statement.
